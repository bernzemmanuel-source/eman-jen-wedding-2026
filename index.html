Got Questions?

Everything You Need to Know

What time should I arrive?

We kindly ask our guests to arrive at least 30 minutes before the ceremony. The Holy Mass will begin promptly at 3:30 PM, with the entourage entrance taking place beforehand.

Can I bring a plus-one?

As we are celebrating an intimate wedding, we can only accommodate guests whose names are included on the invitation. Thank you for understanding.

Can we take photos and videos during the ceremony?

Absolutely! We’d love for you to capture these special moments. However, we kindly ask everyone to remain seated and keep the aisles clear so our official photographers and videographers can capture every moment beautifully.

What is the dress code?

We would love to see you in formal attire! Our wedding motif is Sage and Champagne. For more details, please refer to the Dress Code section of this invitation. Thank you for celebrating with us in style!

Is parking available at the venue?

Yes, parking is available at the venue for guests driving their own vehicles. We recommend arriving early to allow enough time for parking and seating.

What gifts would the couple appreciate?

Your presence is the greatest gift we could ask for. Should you wish to give us an additional blessing, a monetary gift would be warmly appreciated as we begin our journey together.

When is the RSVP deadline?

Kindly confirm your attendance on or before November 20, 2026. Unconfirmed seats after the deadline may be released. For inquiries or RSVP assistance, please contact Eman or Jen.
